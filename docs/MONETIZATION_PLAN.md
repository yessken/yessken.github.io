# Monetization plan: from prototype to first money

## 1. Mission

Turn TUSA from a promising event map into a revenue-generating local ticketing and discovery product for Astana.

The first revenue should come from organizers, not from end users. We do not sell a subscription to visitors. We sell visibility, trust, and ticket conversion.

## 2. The business model

### Core offer
- Discover local events in Telegram
- View event details, venue, price, and remaining seats
- Buy tickets inside the app
- Organizers publish events and receive sales + guest data

### Revenue model
1. Ticket commission: 5–10% on successful ticket sales
2. Event promotion: fixed fee for featured placement in the daily list
3. Organizer toolkit: analytics, QR check-in, guest list, ticket management
4. Partner placements: bars, clubs, and venues pay for visibility

### First-money strategy
Start with one commercial offer only:
- Organizers pay a small fee for featured placement or a ticket commission
- Keep the visitor price simple and transparent
- Use free onboarding for the first 10 organizers to prove conversion

## 3. Product thesis

This is not a general events marketplace. It is a local conversion engine for nightlife and city events in Astana.

The product is valuable when it helps users answer three questions quickly:
- What is happening tonight or this weekend?
- Is it worth going?
- Can I buy a ticket in 30 seconds?

The best clients are organizers who need distribution and ticket conversion.

## 4. First 90-day revenue target

### Month 1: prove demand
- 5 active organizers onboarded
- 20 real events published
- 10 paid tickets sold
- at least 25% conversion from event card to checkout
- at least 20% repeat visitors

### Month 2: operationalize payments
- payment flow live
- ticket status management in place
- organizer dashboard with event and sales data
- 3–5 paid featured promotions sold

### Month 3: repeatable monetization
- 10 active organizers paying monthly or per-event
- 50+ tickets sold
- 2–3 revenue streams active: commission + promotion + toolkit
- first net-positive cohort

## 5. Sales motions

### A. Direct organizer outreach
Target:
- bars
- night clubs
- event agencies
- dance venues
- creative spaces
- small music promoters

Offer:
- free listing for the first 3 events
- assisted setup by the founder
- performance report after the first campaign
- discounted launch price for early partners

### B. Community driven distribution
- Ask event creators to share a custom event link in their Telegram channels
- Let each organizer publish a ready-made promotional message
- Turn promoters into distributed sales agents

### C. Event-led partnerships
- Offer a bundle: event placement + ticket sales + guest list + QR check-in
- Sell to organizers who already have a paying audience

## 6. Commercial offer pack

### Offer 1: Launch listing
- one event published
- event card in the app
- social share link
- price: 0 for first 10 organizers, then 10,000–30,000 KZT or equivalent

### Offer 2: Featured placement
- top section for 24–72 hours
- pinned to discovery feed
- price: 20,000–60,000 KZT depending on reach

### Offer 3: Ticketing + organizer toolkit
- ticket sales enabled
- analytics and guest data
- QR check-in support
- commission: 5–10% on each paid ticket

## 7. Launch sequence

### Week 1–2: closed beta
- recruit 5 organizers manually
- publish only real events
- measure conversion and attendance

### Week 3–4: money flow setup
- integrate payment provider
- add order status flow
- expose ticket QR after payment confirmation
- track organizer sales metrics

### Week 5–8: seller pipeline
- outreach to 20 organizers
- onboard 10 partners
- create a success story for one venue or promoter
- ask for testimonial and referral

### Week 9–12: scale proof
- convert 3–5 regular organizers into monthly clients
- run paid promotion campaigns for selected events
- keep customer acquisition lean and founder-led

## 8. Revenue guardrails

- Do not build a broad lifestyle app before proving organizer demand.
- Do not spend heavily on ads before conversion is proven.
- Do not add premium user subscriptions before first organizer revenue.
- Do not build features without a sales use case.

## 9. What “first money” looks like

The first real money is not one giant contract. It is:
- one organizer paying for visibility
- one event selling tickets via the app
- one promoter returning for another campaign
- one repeat organizer paying again within 30 days

That is the signal we need to keep building.

## 10. Immediate next actions

1. Identify 10 organizer leads and contact them manually.
2. Offer a free launch listing to the first 5 organizers.
3. Finalize the organizer pricing page.
4. Ship payment and ticket status flow.
5. Build a simple organizer dashboard for sales and guest count.
6. Track every funnel metric: page views, checkout starts, successful payments, repeat events.

The project is valuable only when it turns event discovery into verified ticket sales.
