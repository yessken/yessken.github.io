# Sales playbook for first organizers

## Goal

Acquire the first 5–10 organizers who will pay for visibility and conversion.

## Target audience
- nightlife venues
- local promoters
- event agencies
- clubs and bars
- cultural spaces
- organizers with active Telegram channels

## Core pitch

“We help your event reach the right people in Astana and convert interest into tickets inside Telegram, without friction.”

## Outreach script

### Message 1: short and direct

“Hi! I’m building a Telegram-based event discovery app for Astana. We help organizers get more visibility and ticket sales without sending users to a messy chain of links. I’d like to feature your next event and see if it’s a fit. If you’re open, I can set up a free listing for a first event and share the results.”

### Message 2: value-based follow-up

“Most organizers lose buyers because the event is buried in multiple channels. We’re creating a cleaner path: event discovery in Telegram, direct ticket purchase, and a simple organizer dashboard. I’d like to support your next event with a free launch listing and a performance snapshot.”

### Message 3: close

“If you’re interested, I can publish your event this week and share a short link for your audience. We can start with a first campaign, then decide whether to keep the promotion or move to a commission-based model.”

## Sales principles
- Speak in outcomes, not in app features.
- Sell visibility, bookings, and conversion.
- Lead with a first-win offer: free or discounted launch listing.
- Keep the ask small and measurable.

## Objection handling

### “We already have channels.”
Answer: “That is exactly why this helps. We turn your event into a clean, frictionless path to purchase for people already exploring events in Telegram.”

### “We don’t have time.”
Answer: “We can publish the event for you and prepare the sales page. You just provide the details.”

### “What if it doesn’t work?”
Answer: “The first campaign is low-risk. We start with a small test, measure engagement and ticket flow, and only then scale.”

## Conversion checklist

Before closing an organizer:
- event details are confirmed
- date and venue are clear
- ticket price is set
- organizer agrees to share the event link
- measurement plan is defined

## Success metric

The first closed deal is not a great product. It is a validated customer problem and a repeatable sales motion.
